Ferrari F40

The Ferrari F40 stands as the ultimate icon of raw, analog supercar performance, famously serving as the last car personally approved by Enzo Ferrari before his passing and cementing its place in history as a stripped-out, carbon-fibre road racer that lacked any modern driver aids to deliver a terrifyingly pure driving experience powered by a 478-hp twin-turbo V8 that propelled it to become the first production road car to ever breach the 200 mph milestone.

    Key Specifications

  1. Engine:2.9-liter twin-turbocharged 90° V8

  2. Power:478 horsepower (471 hp US-spec) at 7,000 rpm

  3. Torque:426 lb-ft (577 Nm)

  4. Transmission:5-speed manual

  5. Weight:1,100 kg (2,425 lbs)

  6. 0-60MPH (0-100km/h):3.8 to 4.1 seconds

  7. Top Speed:201 mph (324 km/h), making it the first production road car to break the 200 mph barrier.

Shelby Cobra

The Shelby Cobra (specifically the definitive MkIII 427) is a legendary Anglo-American sports car built from 1962 to 1967. It holds massive historical significance as the brainchild of American racing driver Carroll Shelby, who combined a lightweight British AC Ace chassis with a thundering Ford V8 engine. Designed to beat Ferrari on the racetrack, the Cobra was engineered as a brutally powerful, bare-bones roadster that went on to define the classic American muscle era.

    Key Specifications

  1. Engine:7.0-litre (427 cu in) Ford FE V8

  2. Power:425 hp (Standard) / 485 hp (Competition) @ 6,500 RPM

  3. Torque:651 Nm (480 lb-ft) @ 3,500 RPM

  4. Transmission:4-speed manual

  5. Weight:1,068 kg (2,355 lbs) curb weight

  6. 0-60MPH (0-100km/h):4.2 seconds

  7. Top Speed:265 km/h (165 mph)

Lamborghini Countach

The Lamborghini Countach (specifically the definitive LP5000 QV) is a legendary mid-engine, rear-wheel-drive supercar built from 1974 to 1990. It holds massive historical significance as the ultimate archetype of the modern supercar, pioneering the extreme "wedge" design language. Styled by Marcello Gandini at Bertone, it was engineered to shock the automotive world and serve as the direct successor to the beautiful Miura, cementing Lamborghini’s reputation for pure, unadulterated drama.

    Key Specifications

  1. Engine:5.2-litre naturally aspirated 60° V12

  2. Power:455 hp (Euro Spec) / 420 hp (US Spec) @ 7,000 RPM

  3. Torque:500 Nm (369 lb-ft) @ 5,200 RPM

  4. Transmission:5-speed manual

  5. Weight:1,490 kg (3,285 lbs) curb weight

  6. 0-60MPH (0-100km/h):4.8 seconds (0-60 mph in 4.6s)

  7. Top Speed:295 km/h (183 mph)

DeTomaso Pantera

The DeTomaso Pantera (specifically the definitive early 1970s model) is a legendary mid-engine, rear-wheel-drive supercar built from 1971 to 1992. It holds massive historical significance as the ultimate Italian-American hybrid, merging exotic European styling with reliable American horsepower. Designed by Tom Tjaarda at Ghia and engineered by Gianpaolo Dallara, the Pantera was backed by Ford and sold directly through Lincoln-Mercury showrooms to capture a slice of the booming supercar market

    Key Specifications

  1. Engine:5.8-litre (351 cu in) Ford "Cleveland" V8

  2. Power:330 hp @ 5,400 RPM (Gross)

  3. Torque:515 Nm (380 lb-ft) @ 3,400 RPM

  4. Transmission:5-speed ZF manual transaxle

  5. Weight:1,417 kg (3,123 lbs) curb weight

  6. 0-60MPH (0-100km/h):5.5 seconds

  7. Top Speed:256 km/h (159 mph)
